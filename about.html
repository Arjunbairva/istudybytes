ABOUT iSTUDYBYTES

Structured learning, built chapter by chapter.

iStudyBytes is an online educational platform focused on structured CBSE learning resources for Class 9 and Class 10 students. The platform brings chapter-wise study material, practice and revision resources into one organised learning experience.

CBSE 2026–27 Class 9 & 10 Mathematics & Science Chapter-wise

What iStudyBytes is

iStudyBytes is designed to make exam preparation more organised. Instead of treating learning as one large course, the platform structures resources around individual subjects and chapters so students can move from understanding to revision and practice in a clear sequence.

Who creates it

iStudyBytes is created and maintained by Arjun Bairva. The platform combines teaching experience with a focus on clear explanations, chapter-wise organisation and practical exam preparation.

How the learning experience is organised

The platform follows a simple study flow:

  • Understand: learn the concepts using structured chapter resources.
  • Revise: reinforce important ideas using presentations and focused revision resources.
  • Practise: use chapter-wise questions and tests to check understanding.
  • Improve: identify weak areas and return to the relevant chapter resources.

What students can find

  • CBSE Class 9 and Class 10 Mathematics and Science resources.
  • Chapter-wise notes, PPT presentations, one-shot revision resources and tests.
  • Free learning resources, including chapter guides and quizzes.
  • Course access and purchased resources organised through a student dashboard.

Our approach to curriculum

iStudyBytes organises its resources around the intended CBSE curriculum and the textbooks used for the relevant academic session. Curriculum references are provided for educational context; iStudyBytes is not claiming affiliation with or endorsement by CBSE or NCERT.
